🛡️ End-to-End Encryption

Our military-grade encryption ensures your data is protected at every step:

  • AES-256 Encryption: The same encryption standard used by banks and government agencies
  • TLS 1.3: Latest transport layer security for all data transmission
  • Zero-Knowledge Architecture: Even we can't access your encrypted data
  • Key Rotation: Encryption keys are automatically rotated every 90 days
  • Hardware Security Modules: Keys stored in tamper-resistant hardware

All voice data is encrypted immediately upon receipt and remains encrypted during processing, storage, and transmission. Decryption only occurs in secure, isolated environments for AI processing.

📋 HIPAA Compliance Details

Infinity AI is fully compliant with all HIPAA requirements:

  • Business Associate Agreement (BAA): Signed BAA provided to all healthcare clients
  • Administrative Safeguards: Access controls, workforce training, incident response procedures
  • Physical Safeguards: Secure data centers with biometric access controls
  • Technical Safeguards: Encryption, access logging, automatic logoff
  • Regular Audits: Annual compliance audits by certified third parties
  • Staff Training: All personnel complete HIPAA training and background checks

We maintain detailed audit logs of all system access and provide compliance reports upon request. Our legal team stays current with all HIPAA updates and regulatory changes.

🏆 SOC 2 Type II Certification

Our SOC 2 Type II certification validates our commitment to:

  • Security: Information and systems are protected against unauthorized access
  • Availability: Systems are available for operation and use as committed
  • Processing Integrity: System processing is complete, valid, accurate, timely
  • Confidentiality: Information designated as confidential is protected
  • Privacy: Personal information is collected, used, retained, and disposed of properly

This certification involves rigorous testing by independent auditors over a minimum six-month period. Our certification is renewed annually to ensure ongoing compliance.

Audit Firm: Performed by a Big 4 accounting firm with cybersecurity specialization

🔐 Zero Trust Security Architecture

Our Zero Trust model ensures maximum security through:

  • Verify Every User: Multi-factor authentication required for all access
  • Validate Every Device: Device certificates and health checks before network access
  • Limit Access & Privilege: Minimum necessary access with time-based restrictions
  • Learn & Adapt: AI-powered behavioral analysis detects anomalies
  • Assume Breach: Network segmentation limits damage from potential breaches

Every request is authenticated, authorized, and encrypted. Our system continuously monitors for suspicious activity and can instantly revoke access when threats are detected.

Network Segmentation: Critical systems isolated in separate secure zones

Behavioral Analytics: Machine learning algorithms detect unusual access patterns
