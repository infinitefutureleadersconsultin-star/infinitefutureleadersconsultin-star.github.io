### The program — AI Build Lab

- Students build a real, working iOS application from nothing — design, build, debug, and demonstrate.
- Students use AI development tools the way working engineers use them: directing the tool, reading what it produces, and debugging when it does not work.
- Built in **Xcode** on **company-provided Mac laptops**.

### How AI is handled — state this plainly, it is the most important section on the site

- **No student creates an account with any AI company.** Access runs through a single API credential held by the company. No student sign-up, no student credential, no eligibility question.
- **Students work on company laptops, not district machines.** The company brings the machines and takes them back at program close.
- **Usage is controlled and visible on the company side.** The company holds the credential, so it controls what the tool does and what it costs. That cost is the company's, not a district line item.
- **When the program ends, the footprint leaves the building.** Hardware out, no residual accounts, nothing for district staff to decommission.
